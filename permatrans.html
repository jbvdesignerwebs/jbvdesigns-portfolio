P.T. Logo

Tools: Procreate and Adobe Illustrator

Timeline: 3 months (December 2024-March 2025)

Background

This was one of my first major projects outside of working with Ka Manaʻo. I was asked to redesign the logo for PermaTransitions so it would better reflect the organization’s goals, values, and distinct business model centered on accessible, inclusive, and permaculture-based independent facilitation.

Overview

What is PermaTransitions?

PermaTransitions is an independent, career-focused facilitation business based in California. The organization takes a human-centered approach to helping clients navigate changes in their own lives and, when applicable, the lives of their families. Through trauma-informed care, strength-based support, and individualized guidance, PermaTransitions helps clients establish personal goals and work toward achieving them.

Objective

The founders of PermaTransitions were dissatisfied with their original logo and wanted a refreshed identity that more clearly communicated the organization’s mission and brand values. Building on what I learned from the Ka Manaʻo logo redesign, my objective was to develop a more intentional, meaningful, and concise visual identity that reflected the organization’s approach to growth, support, and personal development.

Project Process

Research

To begin, I studied PermaTransitions’ mission, values, and target audience through its website and conversations with the founders.

PermaTransitions supports people of all ages, including individuals with disabilities, by helping them plan and achieve long-term personal and career goals. Its approach combines emotional, educational, and strengths-based support. One of its core philosophies is permaculture, which is used both as a sustainable system and as a metaphor for how individuals can grow independently with the right support.

Based on this understanding, the founders requested that the visual identity incorporate one or more of the following elements: leaves, hands, and/or a tree, representing growth, care, and guidance.

Sketching

With the brand’s values and requested visual elements in mind, I researched simple, symbolic logo styles centered on greenery, growth, and personal development. I intentionally avoided overly abstract or surreal concepts to ensure that the final identity remained clear, approachable, and respectful.

I retained the original color palette from the previous logo because it already aligned with themes of growth, trust, and sustainability. I also aimed to minimize the number of visual elements to prevent unnecessary clutter.

Initially, I explored both text-based and symbol-based logo concepts. However, due to time constraints and the need for a distinctive mark, I focused on developing a strong visual emblem rather than relying heavily on typography.

Most of my sketches centered on tree and plant forms combined with people, hands, and leaves to represent PermaTransitions’ supportive and growth-focused mission. I also explored alternatives without trees, including a growing plant emerging from soil and hands surrounding a leaf.

After presenting multiple concepts, the founders selected the hands surrounding leaves as the strongest direction, with several refinements to develop further.

Drafting

Once a direction was chosen, the founders requested that I combine elements from several earlier sketches. They suggested using simplified human figures as leaf stems to symbolize individuals growing within a supportive system. They also requested a range of cool colors from green to blue, orange accents for contrast and emphasis, and hands that appeared more leaf-like.

I created a refined draft in which the figures were integrated into the leaves, using transparency and subtle variations in shape to improve clarity and distinction.

After reviewing the draft, the founders preferred the hands to appear inside the leaves rather than surrounding them. They also requested that the central leaf use a warmer orange tone to create a focal point. To accommodate this change, I lightened the color of the leaf roots to provide additional contrast with the orange.

I produced two variations: one with visible palms and one without. Ultimately, the founders selected the version without visible palms because its simplified, leaf-like appearance better supported the overall visual identity.

Finalizing

After final approval, I rebuilt the logo in Adobe Illustrator as vector artwork to ensure that it could be reproduced cleanly and consistently at different sizes. I refined the shapes, spacing, and color balance, particularly adjusting the orange leaf so that it felt more natural and cohesive within the overall composition. The final logo was designed to be scalable and flexible for both print and digital applications.

Results

The PermaTransitions logo has been approved as the organization’s official public identity.

More importantly, this project reinforced the importance of designing with thoughtful consideration of a business’s purpose and values. Exploring multiple concepts helped identify the strongest visual direction, while collaborating closely with the founders demonstrated how client feedback can shape and strengthen a rebrand.

PermaTransitions challenged me to create a simple yet respectful identity that communicates growth, support, and sustainability through cohesive visuals.
